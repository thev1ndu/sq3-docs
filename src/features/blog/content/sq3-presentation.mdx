## What is SQ3?

### The Simple Answer

**SQ3 is a unified platform that brings all your customer messages from Facebook, Instagram, and your website into one place, and helps you respond to them faster and smarter.**

Think of it like having a smart assistant that:

- Collects all customer messages from different platforms in one inbox
- Answers common questions automatically
- Understands what customers really want
- Helps you make better business decisions

### Who is SQ3 For?

SQ3 is specifically designed for **Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs)** in Sri Lanka who:

- Use Facebook and Instagram for business
- Have a website with contact forms or chat
- Want to improve customer service
- Need to save time and reduce costs
- Want to grow their business

## The Problem: Why Do We Need SQ3?

### The Challenge Every Business Faces

Imagine running a business where customers message you on:

- Facebook Messenger
- Instagram Direct Messages
- Your website contact form
- Your website live chat

**The problem?** You have to constantly switch between these different platforms to respond to customers. This creates many challenges:

### Problem 1: Too Many Places to Check

**The Situation:**

- You check Facebook messages in the morning
- Instagram messages at lunch
- Website messages in the evening
- But you still miss important messages!

**The Impact:**

- ❌ Customers feel ignored
- ❌ You lose sales opportunities
- ❌ Your team wastes time switching platforms
- ❌ Important messages get missed

### Problem 2: Slow Response Times

**The Situation:**

- Customers send messages, but you don't see them immediately
- By the time you respond, the customer has moved on
- You can't respond when you're busy or after hours

**The Impact:**

- ❌ Customers get frustrated and leave
- ❌ You lose potential sales
- ❌ Your reputation suffers
- ❌ Competitors win your customers

### Problem 3: Answering the Same Questions Again and Again

**The Situation:**

- "What are your opening hours?"
- "Do you deliver to my area?"
- "What are your prices?"
- "How can I place an order?"

Your staff spends hours answering these same questions every day.

**The Impact:**

- ❌ Staff time wasted on repetitive tasks
- ❌ Can't focus on growing the business
- ❌ Inconsistent answers from different staff
- ❌ Higher labor costs

### Problem 4: Not Understanding What Customers Really Want

**The Situation:**

- A customer asks a question, but you're not sure if they want to buy, complain, or just browse
- You treat all messages the same way
- You can't prioritize urgent or important messages

**The Impact:**

- ❌ Missed sales opportunities
- ❌ Unhappy customers not addressed quickly
- ❌ Can't personalize your service
- ❌ Lost revenue

### Problem 5: No Way to Understand Customer Feelings

**The Situation:**

- A customer is unhappy, but you don't know until they leave a bad review
- You can't tell if customers are satisfied or frustrated
- You react to problems instead of preventing them

**The Impact:**

- ❌ Customers leave without you knowing why
- ❌ Bad reviews damage your reputation
- ❌ Can't improve your service proactively
- ❌ Lost customers and revenue

### Problem 6: No Smart Marketing Tools

**The Situation:**

- You want to send marketing messages to customers
- But you don't know who to target
- You send the same message to everyone
- You can't measure if your marketing works

**The Impact:**

- ❌ Ineffective marketing campaigns
- ❌ Wasted marketing budget
- ❌ Customers ignore your messages
- ❌ Low return on marketing investment

### Problem 7: No Insights into Your Business

**The Situation:**

- You don't know how many messages you receive
- You don't know how fast you respond
- You don't know what customers ask about most
- You can't see trends or patterns

**The Impact:**

- ❌ Can't make informed decisions
- ❌ Don't know what's working or not
- ❌ Can't improve your operations
- ❌ Flying blind without data
